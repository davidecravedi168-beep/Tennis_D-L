# Data attribution

Historical ATP/WTA data used by this project is sourced from Jeff Sackmann / Tennis Abstract:

- https://github.com/JeffSackmann/tennis_atp
- https://github.com/JeffSackmann/tennis_wta

The zero-cost research engine currently reads a structured mirror of those archives:

- https://github.com/Aneeshers/tennis-sackmann-archive

This project is intended for personal, analytical, research, and non-commercial use.

Odds and live data come from the configured Odds-API.io account and remain subject to that provider's plan and licensing terms. Commercial mode is blocked unless commercial odds and history licenses are explicitly confirmed through repository variables.
